# Systems

The match's systems in their run order, as the game registers them (each registration's comment says why it sits there); with `Engine:Benchmark:Enabled` the benchmark's systems join them. Generated from the code by the reference tests; do not edit it by hand (`docs/reference/README.md`).

## Fixed, each step

| | System | From | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `OriginShiftSystem` | engine | Keeps the match's origin near the active camera (fixed; a game registers it first, so the whole step runs in one origin, the only place in a step it may move): when the camera is `Engine:Origin:ShiftMetres` or more from the origin along any axis, the origin moves to the camera's whole metres (`OriginShifts`), and everything kept in local positions with it; a jump the step will make (`tp`, `OriginShifts.RequestFollow`) goes first, in place of the camera. |
| 2 | `PlayerSpawnSystem` | game | Makes the local player at the match's start (fixed group, first; it does nothing after): an upright body of the options' height, standing under the start pose's eye and looking where it looks, with every component the engine's player systems read and a `Climber` (the engine's `ClimbingMode`). |
| 3 | `FoxSystem` | game | The fox (`GameModels.Fox`) (fixed group, after `PlayerSpawnSystem`, before the engine's `MeshAnimatorSystem`, which moves its clip on). |
| 4 | `MapResetSystem` | engine | The console's `cleanup` and `reset` (fixed group, early: after the origin's system and the game's spawns; `CommandScope.Match`): the command asks, and the next step starts with the work. |
| 5 | `DebugBallSystem` | engine | The console's `balls` (fixed group, before `PhysicsReadSystem`; `CommandScope.Match`): a debug aid that drops balls of one material, made from nothing, in a row across the view 3 m in front of the `ActiveCamera`, at the next step with one. |
| 6 | `PlayerInputSystem` | engine | Builds the local player's `PlayerCommand` from the fixed step's input (fixed group, before every system that reads the command), by the movement keybindings (`MovementKeys`; the defaults in brackets): Forward, Back, Left, and Right to move (W, S, A, D), Sprint (Shift) and Crouch (Ctrl) while held, Jump (Space, once a press), and while the pointer is captured the mouse's movement since the last step added to the look. |
| 7 | `PhysicsReadSystem` | engine | Reads what the last physics step did (fixed group, right after the input; the physics step runs after the group): each loose body's pivot and orientation into `BodyPose`, the pose before it kept as the previous one for drawing between steps; and the push loose bodies gave each character's stand-in (`PhysicsWorld.PushOn`), which goes to `CharacterVelocity.Push` for the movement system and is kept, with its impulse, in `CharacterStandIn`. |
| 8 | `PlayerMovementSystem` | engine | Moves players through the voxels (fixed group, after the input), each by its `MovementStats`: turns the horizontal velocity towards the one the command asks for (faster on walkable ground than in the air, where only keys steer and no keys keep the speed), jumps from walkable ground, adds gravity, and hands the move to `VoxelCharacterMover`, which slides the body along walls, steps it onto ledges, and lands it. |
| 9 | `StandInSystem` | engine | Keeps each character's stand-in (fixed group, after the tools, before `LooseBodySystem`): a character gets one while a thrown ball (not a `CutPiece`, which touches only the voxels) comes within `StandInSystem.NearMetres` of its middle, and loses it when none does (and while a player flies the debug camera), so stand-ins cost nothing when no such body is around. |
| 10 | `LooseBodySystem` | engine | Ends loose bodies (fixed group, after the tools): a body at rest (`LooseBodies.IsAtRest`) for `Engine:Physics:SettleSeconds` in a row goes back into the voxel grid where it lies (`LooseBodies.Settle`), unless its bounds reach into a character's cylinder (the piece would fill the air the character stands in); it waits then. |
| 11 | `MeshAnimatorSystem` | engine | Advances each `MeshAnimator`'s clip time (fixed group, before the systems that read a pose, after those that choose clips): the time moves to the previous, and on by `MeshAnimator.Speed` times the step, so clips follow the match's time and stop while it is paused. |
| 12 | `StreamingCentreSystem` | engine | Centres voxel streaming on the local player's eye (fixed group, last), so the voxels follow the player even while no frames are drawn. |

## Frame, each frame drawn

| | System | From | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `PlayerCameraSystem` | engine | Puts the `ActiveCamera` at the local player's eye (frame group, first) while it walks: the feet and the eye's ease drawn between the last two fixed steps by `FrameTime.Alpha`, looking where the command looks plus the mouse movement since the last step, which the next step will add, so looking stays smooth at any frame rate. |
| 2 | `DebugFlyCameraSystem` | engine | The debug fly camera (frame group, after the player's camera, which makes the camera entity). |
| 3 | `DebugOutlineSystem` | engine | Outlines loose bodies while the debug lines are on (`lines`; frame group, before `CameraExtractSystem`): each body's piece box, turned with it, and each character's stand-in cylinder while it has one, between their last two poses by `FrameTime.Alpha`. |
| 4 | `CameraExtractSystem` | engine | Hands the `ActiveCamera` to the renderer (`RenderView`) (frame group, registered after every system that moves the camera, so it sees the frame's final pose). |
| 5 | `MinimapSystem` | game | Draws the minimap (frame group, after the camera's systems): its key (`Minimap.Key`, M) turns its switch on and off, unless the player's input is blocked; while it is on, and not in the benchmark, it asks for the minimap's image view each frame (`RenderViews.Draw`) from an orthographic camera at the local player's eye looking straight down, north up, `Game:Minimap:Metres` across, and tells the minimap's layer it shows and where the player looks (`Minimap.Shown`, `Minimap.Yaw`). |
| 6 | `LooseBodyModelSystem` | engine | Hands every loose body's models to the renderer (`ModelInstances`) (frame group, after `CameraExtractSystem`, whose camera picks each cut piece's level, and before any system of the game that adds more models: loose bodies come first, so when more models stand about than a frame holds (`ModelInstances.MaxModels`) they are never the ones left out). |
| 7 | `AtmosphereSystem` | engine | Sets the frame's sky, sunlight, and air, its look, and its haze glows, from an `IAtmosphereSource` at the camera (frame group, after `CameraExtractSystem`, whose camera it reads from `RenderView`). |
| 8 | `MeshDrawSystem` | engine | Hands every placed mesh asset to the renderer (`MeshInstances`) (frame group, after `CameraExtractSystem`, the systems that ask for image views or set the atmosphere, and `LooseBodyModelSystem`): each entity with a `MeshPlacement` and a `DrawnMesh` is drawn where it is between the last two fixed steps, its pose blended by `FrameTime.Alpha` and scaled; one that also has a `MeshAnimator` is posed by its clip at the blend of its last two times (its rig's skinning matrices, `Rig.WritePalette`), and while it fades from another clip by both, each joint blended by the fade (rotations along the shorter way); one with a `ShapedPose` is then shaped by the game's `IMeshPoseShaper`, and one with a `SmoothedPose` has its changes smoothed as it is drawn, after the shaping (`PoseSmoothing`: each starts from the pose drawn the frame before, moving as it was). |
