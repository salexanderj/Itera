# The engine's options

Every option under `Engine`: its default, the least and most its validator takes (each tried alone from the defaults; its text names any values between that it refuses), and what it does. A game's `appsettings.json` may ship other values (its `game-options.md`). Generated from the code by the reference tests; do not edit it by hand (`docs/reference/README.md`).

| Option | Default | Least to most | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Engine:Application:Name` | `Itera` |  | The name players see: the text drawn on screen, the log header, and the application name drivers see. |
| `Engine:Application:DataFolder` | `Itera` |  | The folder that holds the logs, saves, settings, and caches: a name of letters, digits, '-', and '_' only, under the per-user local application data folder, or a full path whose last folder is such a name. Log files are named after that name in lower case. |
| `Engine:Window:Title` | `Itera` |  | Window title. |
| `Engine:Window:Width` | `1280` | `1` to `32768` | Initial width in logical pixels. |
| `Engine:Window:Height` | `720` | `1` to `32768` | Initial height in logical pixels. |
| `Engine:Window:VSync` | `true` |  | When true the swapchain uses FIFO presentation. When false it prefers mailbox. |
| `Engine:Window:Fullscreen` | `false` |  | When true the window covers its screen. The menu's settings change it (and keep it in settings.json). |
| `Engine:Window:Screen` | empty |  | The screen full screen covers, by the name the system gives it (such as `DP-1`), at most 128 characters; empty (the default) for the system's main screen, which is also used while no screen of that name is connected. The menu's settings change it (and keep it in settings.json). |
| `Engine:Window:Mode` | `Window` | `Window`, `Offscreen`, `None` | `WindowMode.Window` (the default): an OS window. `WindowMode.Offscreen`: no window; the game draws as usual at `WindowSettings.Width` x `WindowSettings.Height` pixels into a headless surface (`VK_EXT_headless_surface`), which nothing shows, for screenshots and checks that must not open a window. Input comes only from the control port. |
| `Engine:Window:OffscreenFps` | `240` | `1` to `1000` | Frames a second drawn at most without a window (nothing paces a headless surface), 1 to 1000. The world's meshes are read and uploaded a share each frame, so a low cap slows loading: at 60 the starter's view did not load in 15 s. |
| `Engine:Renderer:MeshFinestLevel` | `0` | `0` to `9` | The finest level the world is meshed and drawn at, 0 to 9: 0 by default, the game's 10 cm voxels. Finer levels than it still stream for the mover and physics; above the coarsest level streamed, the coarsest is meshed alone. |
| `Engine:Renderer:MeshReachShare` | `0.5` | `0.05` to `1` | How far each level is drawn, as a share of its streaming reach (`Engine:Voxels:Level0ReachMetres`, `ReachGrowth`), 0.05 to 1; the rest of the reach is room to load before the level is drawn. At 0.5 (the default) the game's 10 cm voxels are drawn to 16.8 m, 168 voxels, where they are 5 to 7 pixels wide at 1440p, and each coarser level twice as far. |
| `Engine:Renderer:ObjectVoxelPixels` | `2` | `0.5` to `16` | How wide on the drawn image one voxel of a voxel object's model must be, in pixels, for that level to be drawn, 0.5 to 16: 2 by default. An object (`Objects/`) draws its finest level while one of its own voxels covers at least this many pixels at its nearest point, then each coarser level while one of that level's voxels does, so its detail follows its voxel size, the screen's height, and the lens, not the terrain's levels. At 2 and 1440p with a 90 degree view, 10 cm objects draw their own voxels to about 64 m and 20 cm ones to about 128 m. Larger numbers draw coarser models nearer: fewer triangles, softer far shapes. |
| `Engine:Renderer:ViewDistanceMetres` | `256` | `0` to `8000` | The view distance in metres, where the fog is whole and the world's meshes stop: 256 by default, 0 to 8000; 0 for the coarsest streamed level's radius (473.6 m with four levels), which also ends a longer one. 8000 is the farthest measured (nine levels on the starter's 8 km map; ARCHITECTURE.md). |
| `Engine:Renderer:MsaaSamples` | `4` | `1` to `8` | Samples per pixel the world is drawn with: 1 (no MSAA), 2, 4 (the default), or 8, lowered to what the device offers. With more than one, SMAA is not run. The settings' Anti-aliasing changes it at once. |
| `Engine:Renderer:MeshMegabytes` | `512` | `64` to `4096` | Megabytes of GPU memory for the meshes, the world's and the models', 64 to 4096, taken once at start: 512 by default. It is lowered to the device's storage buffer range, its largest buffer, and half its largest device-local memory heap. When it is full, the meshes of columns the view does not draw are freed first; past that, a block waits without a mesh (warned once a match). |
| `Engine:Renderer:SkinMegabytes` | `16` | `1` to `512` | Megabytes of GPU memory a frame slot takes for the posed mesh assets' skinned vertices, 1 to 512, taken once at start and only when a skinned asset was read: 16 by default (about 466,000 vertices, 270 of the starter's fox). It is lowered to fit the device's storage buffer range beside every asset's own vertices. Posed instances past it are drawn unposed (warned once a run). |
| `Engine:Renderer:ShadowMapTexels` | `2048` | `512` to `4096` | Texels along each side of each sun shadow cascade's depth map, 512 to 4096: 2048 by default. Fewer texels draw and filter faster, with blurrier and blockier shadow edges. |
| `Engine:Renderer:DistantShadowMetres` | `0` | `0` to `8000` | The view depth in metres where a third sun shadow cascade, past the far one's 200 m, ends: 0 (the default) leaves it out; else 400 to 8000, ended sooner by the view distance (with a view that ends before the far cascade does, it is never drawn, though its map's memory is still taken). It shows the shadows of hills and large shapes in the distance under a low sun: it is drawn only while the sun is under 30 degrees over the horizon, fading in down to 25 (a higher sun's shadows are too short to see that far), and only the terrain casts into it, not the models or mesh assets. Its map is one more layer of the shadow maps (`RendererSettings.ShadowMapTexels` squared, 16 MB at 2048), its texels about 2.4 m wide at 2000 m with a 90 degree view. |
| `Engine:Renderer:MostLights` | `128` | `1` to `256` | The light budget: point lights a frame shades with, 1 to 256 (`Rendering.Meshes.MeshPass.MostLights`), 128 by default: the glowing blocks' and the events' (`Rendering.PointLights`) brightest at the camera. Each pixel loops over only the lights of its cluster of the view. |
| `Engine:Renderer:PreferredDevice` | none |  | Substring of a physical device name to prefer. Null selects by device type. |
| `Engine:Renderer:Validation` | none |  | Enable Vulkan validation layers. Null means "on in Debug builds, off otherwise". |
| `Engine:Renderer:SyncValidation` | `true` |  | Add synchronization validation (hazards between commands, queues, and the swapchain) when validation is on. |
| `Engine:Renderer:BestPracticesValidation` | `true` |  | Add the layer's best-practices checks when validation is on. Vendor-specific checks stay off. |
| `Engine:Renderer:SkyRed` | `0.55` | `0` to `1` | Red component of the sky's colour at the horizon, linear, 0 to 1. The sky, with green and blue, of a game that sets no atmosphere of its own (`RenderView`'s clear sky). The engine's `AtmosphereSystem` sets one every frame from a game's `IAtmosphereSource`, so with a source these three show only on frames the loading screen covers. |
| `Engine:Renderer:SkyGreen` | `0.68` | `0` to `1` | Green component of the sky's colour at the horizon, linear, 0 to 1 (see `RendererSettings.SkyRed`). |
| `Engine:Renderer:SkyBlue` | `0.85` | `0` to `1` | Blue component of the sky's colour at the horizon, linear, 0 to 1 (see `RendererSettings.SkyRed`). |
| `Engine:Renderer:UploadMegabytesPerFrame` | `16` | `4` to `256` | Megabytes of staging memory per frame slot: the most data the CPU can hand the GPU to copy in one frame. Work that does not fit waits for the next frame. |
| `Engine:Renderer:Shadows` | `true` |  | Whether the sun casts shadows through the shadow maps. Off saves their drawing (0.5 to 1.8 ms at 2560x1440 on an RX 5700 XT, docs/rendering-redesign.md); every surface facing the sun is then lit. |
| `Engine:Renderer:RenderScale` | `1` | `0.5` to `1` | The share of the screen's size, on each axis, that the world and models are drawn at while `RendererSettings.DynamicRenderScaling` is off, 0.5 to 1 (the benchmark and tests set a scale so); AMD's FSR 1 upscales and sharpens the picture to the screen. At 1 the picture is drawn straight onto the screen, with no upscale. The console's `scale` sets a scale for the run. |
| `Engine:Renderer:AntiAliasing` | `true` |  | Whether the picture is anti-aliased with SMAA 1x before FSR 1 upscales it (`Rendering.SmaaPass`) when the world is drawn with one sample a pixel (`RendererSettings.MsaaSamples` 1): 0.23 to 0.31 ms at 2560x1440. It smooths the steps along edges, not what is thinner than a pixel. The settings' Anti-aliasing changes it, and the console's `aa` for the run. |
| `Engine:Renderer:DynamicRenderScaling` | `true` |  | Dynamic render scaling: the render scale, 0.5 to 1, follows the GPU's time per frame so the frame rate holds the lower of `RendererSettings.DynamicScalingThreshold` and the screen's refresh rate (`DynamicRenderScale`); at 1 with time to spare it stays at 1. Off, the world is drawn at `RendererSettings.RenderScale`. The settings' Dynamic render scaling changes it. |
| `Engine:Renderer:DynamicScalingThreshold` | `120` | `30` to `360` | The frame rate dynamic render scaling holds, 30 to 360 frames a second; the settings' Dynamic scaling threshold offers the common refresh rates (`DynamicRenderScale.Thresholds`). |
| `Engine:Renderer:DebugLines` | `false` |  | Starts with the debug lines on (outlines of loose bodies and their collision cells); the console's `lines` turns them off and on. |
| `Engine:Renderer:ShaderSourceDirectory` | none |  | The folder the shaders are compiled from; null for `Shaders/` next to the executable. Pointed at the repository's `src/Itera.Engine/Rendering/Shaders`, the console's `shaders reload` shows a shader edit in the running game. |
| `Engine:Renderer:GameShaderSourceDirectory` | none |  | The folder a game's own shaders are compiled from (the paths under `Game/`); null for `Shaders/Game/` next to the executable. Pointed at the game's `Shaders` folder in the repository, `shaders reload` shows an edit of a game's shader too. |
| `Engine:Mods:Enabled` | `true` |  | False to load no mods: a game that does not take code mods yet runs no code it did not ship, whatever lies in `ModsSettings.Directory`. True, the default, loads them. |
| `Engine:Mods:Directory` | none |  | Folder that holds one sub-folder per mod. Null means "Mods" next to the executable. |
| `Engine:Logging:Directory` | none |  | Folder that receives one log file per run. Null means "Logs" in the application's data folder. |
| `Engine:Logging:FileEnabled` | `true` |  | When false no log file is written. The console sink still runs. |
| `Engine:Logging:ConsoleLevel` | `Information` | `Trace`, `Debug`, `Information`, `Warning`, `Error`, `Critical`, `None` | Lowest level shown on the console. The file receives every level the global minimum allows. |
| `Engine:Logging:FileSizeLimitMegabytes` | `50` | `1` to `1048576` | Size at which the current file rolls over to a numbered continuation file. |
| `Engine:Logging:RetainedFiles` | `20` | `1` to `100000` | Newest log files kept when the game starts. Older files beyond this count are deleted. |
| `Engine:Logging:RetainedDays` | `30` | `0` to `100000` | Log files older than this many days are deleted when the game starts. Zero disables the age check. |
| `Engine:Logging:StatsIntervalSeconds` | `10` | `0` to `86400` | Seconds between loop health summaries. Zero disables them. |
| `Engine:Voxels:StreamingRadiusMetres` | `0` | `0` to `500` | Columns that reach within this horizontal distance of a streaming centre are loaded at full detail (level 0, the world grid's voxels: 10 cm in the game), unless `VoxelSettings.Level0ReachMetres` sets level 0's distance. 0 (the default) takes it from the grid (`VoxelSettings.StreamingRadiusMetresFor`): 59.2 m, or 4.625 level-0 columns where that is farther (voxels over 10 cm: 592 m at 1 m); at 10 cm level 0 then reaches 33.6 m, two 12.8 m columns less, and is drawn to half that (`Engine:Renderer:MeshReachShare`). The streamed world reaches this times 2^(`VoxelSettings.Levels` - 1); `Engine:Renderer:ViewDistanceMetres` ends the view sooner. |
| `Engine:Voxels:Levels` | `4` | `1` to `10` | Levels of detail streamed, 1 to 10: level k has voxels 2^k times the grid's (10, 20, 40, and 80 cm in the game) and loads columns within `VoxelSettings.StreamingRadiusMetres` times 2^k (at the default `VoxelSettings.ReachGrowth`). Four (the default) reach 473.6 m at the default radius on 10 cm voxels, the coarsest giving the far meshes; the sky field needs the level below the one nearest 64 cm (level 2 in the game). |
| `Engine:Voxels:RemotePlayerRadiusMetres` | `16` | `2` to `256` | The radius of the level-0 disc a session host streams around each remote player, in metres (2 to 256): the host moves a client's player from its commands, and the mover waits where the voxels round the feet are not loaded, so the disc holds what the player can reach before the columns ahead load (16 m: two seconds at a sprint). |
| `Engine:Voxels:Level0ReachMetres` | `0` | `0` to `113.27499999999998` | How far the view draws level 0, in metres; its columns load two of them (25.6 m in the game) farther. 0 draws it to the streaming radius less those two columns (33.6 m at 59.2 m). Other streaming centres (a host's discs round remote players and bodies) keep their own radii. |
| `Engine:Voxels:ReachGrowth` | `2` | `1.25` to `2` | How many times farther the view draws each level than the one before (`LevelDistances`), from 1.25 to 2: below 2 the detail falls off faster with distance, and levels 1 up to the coarsest load less. The coarsest level is drawn to the end of the view whatever this is. |
| `Engine:Voxels:MemoryBudgetMegabytes` | `1024` | `64` to `65536` | Native memory voxel storage may take. At the cap, streaming stops loading and logs a warning; it loads again once 5 % of the cap has been freed. |
| `Engine:Voxels:WorkerThreads` | `0` | `0` to `64` | Threads that generate chunks. Zero means half the processors, at least one. |
| `Engine:Voxels:Seed` | `1` | any | World seed of a match that does not name one (a save's seed or a start command's seed argument overrides it). A joining client takes its host's. |
| `Engine:Voxels:DebugWalkSpeed` | `0` | `0` to `100` | Debug aid for unattended runs: when above zero, the debug camera ignores input and flies a circle of 100 m radius around the origin, 12 m up, at this many metres per second, and voxel streaming follows it. Zero leaves the camera to the keyboard and mouse. |
| `Engine:Voxels:EditColumnsPerStep` | `4` | `1` to `64` | Columns each fixed step applies large edits (a 4 m sphere, an explosion) to; the rest wait for the next steps (`VoxelWorld.ApplyPendingEdits`). Measured in Release in the real world: a 4 m dig reaches about 78 columns at about 1.4 ms each, a 4 m place about 73 at about 2.6 ms, so at 4 a step takes about 5 to 6 ms for a dig and 10 for a place, near the old 8 ms. |
| `Engine:Camera:FieldOfViewDegrees` | `90` | `30` to `120` | Horizontal field of view in degrees on a 16:9 screen (90 gives about 59 degrees vertically). The vertical angle stays the same on every screen, so a wider window sees more to the sides and a narrower one less. |
| `Engine:Camera:FlySpeed` | `10` | `0.1` to `1000` | Starting speed of the debug fly camera in metres per second. The mouse wheel changes it. |
| `Engine:Camera:MouseSensitivity` | `0.1` | `0.001` to `10` | Mouse look: degrees the camera turns per pixel of mouse movement. |
| `Engine:Camera:CircleHeightMetres` | `20` | `-1000` to `1000` | Height of the automatic circle the debug camera flies (`Engine:Voxels:DebugWalkSpeed`, and the benchmark's flight), in metres: it must clear the game's ground along a 100 m circle round the origin. |
| `Engine:Origin:ShiftMetres` | `512` | `8` to `4096` | How far, in metres along any axis, the active camera may go from the match's origin before the origin moves to it (`OriginShiftSystem`): 512 by default, 8 to 4096. | `Engine:Navigation:WindowCells` | `1024` | `256` to `4096` | The most cells of 50 cm the navigation grid's window is across: 1024 (512 m) by default, 256 to 4096 (under 256 a window moving by 64-cell tiles would move only once its focus had left it). | `Engine:Physics:CollisionCentimetres` | `10` | `1` to `10` | The edge of the character mover's cells in a match without voxel terrain, in centimetres, 1 to `PhysicsSettings.MostCollisionCentimetres`: it reads the mesh colliders as columns of these cells, and on cells coarser than 1 cm walks their slopes as ramps. | `Engine:Physics:TerrainCellCentimetres` | `0` | `0` to `100` | The edge of the character mover's cells over voxel terrain, in centimetres: 0 (the default) for the world's level-0 voxels; else a whole divisor of them, 1 to `PhysicsSettings.MostTerrainCellCentimetres` (a match whose world's voxels it does not divide does not start). | `Engine:Physics:Substeps` | `1` | `1` to `8` | The solver's substeps in each fixed step, 1 to `PhysicsSettings.MostSubsteps`: more keep fast, stacked, or jointed bodies steadier, at more cost (Bepu solves the constraints, and moves the bodies that have any, once a substep).
